To activate the real CV/résumé download buttons on the site:
1. Export your current CV and résumé (from RicardoPavan_CV_2026.docx /
   RicardoPavan_Resume_2026.docx) to PDF.
2. Save them as:
     files/cv/RicardoPavan_CV_2026.pdf
     files/cv/RicardoPavan_Resume_2026.pdf
   (these exact paths are already referenced in _variables.yml)
3. Delete this legacy-reference-only folder once you've confirmed the new PDFs work.
